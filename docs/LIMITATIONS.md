# Simulated features, assumptions and known limitations

## Simulated in this prototype

| Feature | How it is simulated |
|---|---|
| Personnel, visitors, contractors, vehicles, deliveries | Entirely **synthetic** records generated by `server/src/seed/demo.ts` (fictional names, `TCP-*` personnel IDs, `DM …` plates, "(Demo)" organizations). |
| RFID/NFC credentials | Credential numbers are typed or produced by a **mock reader** button. No reader hardware is connected; see [INTEGRATIONS.md](INTEGRATIONS.md). |
| QR scanning | Real QR codes are generated and can be scanned with a device camera (HTTPS or localhost required), but the **simulated scanner** list and manual reference entry are provided for demonstrations. |
| Gate verification | Server-side checks against the synthetic database only. **Not connected to any Army or establishment access-control system.** No barrier, turnstile or door is ever actuated. |
| Notifications | **In-app only.** No SMS, email or push messages are sent. "Read" is recorded only when a user opens the notification in the app. |
| Security verification step (visitors) | A recorded checkpoint ("step completed by X at Y with note") — it does not perform or represent any background check. |
| Delivery authorization | Records a decision to let a delivery proceed **under the establishment's applicable procedures**; it is not proof of inspection or acceptance. |
| Vehicle verification | Lookup against the vehicle register only. **No automatic number-plate recognition** and no physical vehicle inspection. |
| Historical activity | The seed back-fills a week of plausible movement, alerts and exceptions so dashboards and reports are populated. |

## Assumptions (documented defaults, all configurable)

The proposal document and demonstration video referenced in the brief were not available in the development environment, so the visual design follows the written brief (deep forest green, muted gold, institutional). Where client rules were unknown, safe configurable defaults were used:

- **Visitor statuses:** the spec's statuses are used; "awaiting authorization" covers Expected, Arrived, Host Notified and Waiting. Transitions are editable under *Workflow Settings* and enforced by the server.
- **Segregation of duties:** a requester cannot approve their own visitor request (`visit.allowSelfApproval = false`).
- **Passes:** single-use (one entry, one exit), valid from 60 minutes before expected arrival to 60 minutes after expected departure.
- **Overdue:** 30 minutes after expected departure. **Delivery acknowledgement alert:** 4 hours after authorization. **Unresolved exception alert:** 24 hours.
- **Deliveries require authorization** after arrival; guards cannot authorize unless enabled.
- **Manual review** may be requested only for: Manual Review Required, Outside Permitted Hours, Wrong Location, Expired Access/Credential, Inactive Credential, Not Yet Valid. Blocked/lost credentials, cancelled passes and unapproved visits cannot be referred.
- **Exit is always recordable** for someone recorded inside at that location, even if their access has since expired, been revoked or is otherwise invalid — except blocked or lost credentials, which require manual handling.
- **Personnel "outside"** = active personnel assigned to the location who are not currently recorded inside (including those with no recorded movement).
- **Time zone:** `Asia/Kolkata` for calendar dates, permitted hours and report day boundaries (`APP_TIMEZONE`).
- **Ranks/designations** are free text with synthetic values; no real unit names are used.

## Not implemented / known limitations

- **No operational integrations:** no live personnel database, identity provider/SSO, SMS/email gateway, CCTV, ANPR, biometrics or access-control hardware.
- **Single-node deployment:** SQLite, in-process session store and rate limiter. Multi-node operation needs PostgreSQL and a shared store (see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- **Real-time updates** use polling (15–30 s) rather than push.
- **PDF export** is provided through the browser's *Print → Save as PDF* with print-friendly styles; there is no server-generated PDF.
- **Photographs** are not stored; generated initials avatars are shown instead.
- **Data retention, archival and legal hold** policies are not implemented.
- **Multi-factor authentication** is not implemented.
- **Offline operation** of the gate interface is not supported; the gate needs network access to the server.
- **Localization:** English only.
- **Camera QR scanning** depends on browser support and secure context; the simulated scanner and manual entry remain available.
- **Manual reference entry** accepts sequential references (e.g. `VR-2026-00042`) in place of the signed QR; it is recorded as a separate method but should be restricted by policy before operational use.
- **Cross-location visitor identity search** is limited to visitors with visits in the user's own locations, so the same person may be registered separately at two locations.
- The prototype is **not security-certified or approved for operational use**.
